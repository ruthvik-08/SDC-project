Regional clustering demonstrates that uniform irrigation protocols are inefficient across India. High-efficiency micro-sprinklers must be prioritized for Zone 2 (Arid Deficit Belt), furrow irrigation for Zone 0 (Central Belt), and drainage canal regulation for Zone 1 (Coastal Belt).
